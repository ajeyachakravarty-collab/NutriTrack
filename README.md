# NutriTrack — Free Indian Food & Calorie Tracker

A 100% free, privacy-first web app for tracking Indian meals, calories, macros, water intake, and workouts—with no paywalls, ads, or backend servers.

**Live App:** [https://ajeyachakravarty-collab.github.io/NutriTrack/](https://ajeyachakravarty-collab.github.io/NutriTrack/)

## Features
- **Calorie & Macro Budget:** Interactive daily calorie ring and Protein / Carbs / Fat progress bars across five meal slots (Breakfast, Morning Snack, Lunch, Evening Snack, Dinner).
- **Indian Food Database:** 55+ everyday Indian and global dishes using standardized *katori* (150 ml), *roti*, and piece portions grounded in [HealthifyMe Nutrifacts](https://www.healthifyme.com/web/nutrifacts/chapati-68165d7700) and the [YourTrainer Indian Food Calorie Chart](https://www.yourtrainer.in/blog/indian-food-calories-macro-chart-60-dishes).
- **Snap Food (Photo Logging):** Capture or upload a meal photo with automatic compression, offline Smart Plate Scanner matching, live [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/) search, or optional Gemini AI Vision recognition using your own free Google AI Studio key.
- **Edit & Custom Foods:** Edit any logged item's name, serving size, calories, and macros, or save custom dishes.
- **Water, Workouts & Analytics:** Track daily water glasses, workout calorie burn, 7-day calorie trends, and macro splits.
- **TDEE Calculator & Backups:** Built-in Mifflin-St Jeor TDEE calculator and one-click JSON data export.

## Privacy
All meal logs, custom foods, goals, and optional API keys are stored locally in your browser (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent to any third-party server unless you explicitly use **Search Online** (Open Food Facts) or **AI Vision** (Google Gemini API).

## Install on Mobile (Chrome)
1. Open [https://ajeyachakravarty-collab.github.io/NutriTrack/](https://ajeyachakravarty-collab.github.io/NutriTrack/) in Chrome.
2. Tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install** (or **Create shortcut**).
